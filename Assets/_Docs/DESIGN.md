# Design Intent — Opening Level

## Goal
Deliver a polished, playable 2D side-scrolling opening level that establishes the world of Bastion Zero and the character Nexus before the full catastrophe.

## Player Fantasy
Nexus is exploring a once-peaceful, tree-covered planet after the rest of their explorer team has been separated or lost. The level should feel beautiful but quietly wrong — lush forest with subtle signs of unease.

## Core Verbs (this level only)
- Move and jump with satisfying platformer feel
- Traverse platforms, gaps, and light hazards
- Solve one simple environmental puzzle
- Find and collect hidden items
- Reach a clear exit

## Non-Goals (do not implement yet)
- Summons (Vesper, Archon, Talos)
- Combat or enemies
- Full story cutscenes or dialogue
- Multiple regions or the ruined/ash aesthetic
- Stat upgrades beyond basic collectible pickup

## Success Criteria
A player can load the scene, control Nexus smoothly, complete a 2–4 minute level containing platforms, one puzzle, and several collectibles, then trigger a level-complete state.
